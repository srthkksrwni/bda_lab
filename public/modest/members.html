MODEST

Multimodal Analysis for Mental Disorder Recognition to Improve Well-being

Team & Governance

The project is hosted at the Department of Information Technology, Indian Institute of Information Technology (IIIT) - Allahabad Funded by CSTUP.

Funding Agency

Government of Uttar Pradesh

Program Leadership: Ms. Pooja Yadav

Principal Investigator: Prof. Sonali Agarwal

Prof. Sonali Agarwal is a Professor at IIIT Allahabad with a Ph.D. in the field of Big Data and Machine Learning. Her research currently focuses on the extensions of Support Vector Machines, including Least Squares and Twin SVMs, as well as Stream Analytics for Big Data environments. As the Lead of the MODEST project, she leverages her expertise in Software Metrics and IT Infrastructure to oversee the transformation of conventional algorithms into scalable, multimodal mental health diagnostic tools.

Academic Focus: Stream Analytics, Big Data Analytics, Complex Event Processing, Machine Learning.

Email: sonali@iiita.ac.in
Co-Investigator: Prof. Vrijendra Singh

Prof. Vrijendra Singh is a Professor at IIIT Allahabad with a Ph.D. in Independent Component Analysis and Blind Source Separation. His expertise spans Biomedical Analysis, Artificial Neural Networks, and Digital Signal Processing. In the MODEST project, he specializes in separating complex signal mixtures, audio enhancement, and image denoising, which are critical for the framework's multi-sensory diagnostic accuracy.

Academic Focus: Data Mining & Machine Learning, Digital Signal & Image Processing, Information Security & Forensics.

Email: vrij@iiita.ac.in
Co-Investigator: Dr. Narinder Singh

Dr. Narinder Singh is an Assistant Professor at ABV-IIITM Gwalior. His research interests include Mathematical Modeling, Numerical Analysis, and Computational Fluid Dynamics. In the MODEST project, he provides expertise in the mathematical modeling of complex multimodal datasets and algorithmic optimization for diagnostic accuracy.

Email: narinder@iiitm.ac.in
Co-Investigator: Prof. Karm Veer Arya

Prof. Karm Veer Arya is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at ABV-IIITM Gwalior. With over 34 years of experience and a Ph.D. from IIT Kanpur, he is a distinguished researcher in Image Processing, AI, and Machine Learning. He has published over 200 papers and authored several books on intelligent vision and image analysis, bringing high-level expertise in technical framework validation to the MODEST project.

Email: kvarya@iiitm.ac.in
Collaborators & Stakeholders

The team actively engages with clinical domain experts, caregivers, and professional psychologists to ensure the MODEST framework meets real-world clinical standards and provides actionable insights.

"We are building a space where the multimodal (the technical) meets the modest (the humble, human-centric approach), allowing for a radical rediscovery of the self"

Project Identity

Multimodal Analysis for Mental Disorder Recognition to Improve Mental Health and Well-being.An initiative at the BDA Lab, IIIT Allahabad, aimed at integrating AI-driven diagnostics with culturally grounded perspectives to support more accessible, equitable, and context-sensitive mental health solutions
