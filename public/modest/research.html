Research & Publications

Advancing Mental Health Informatics through Multimodal Fusion

1. Research Focus & Methodology

Our research moves away from single-source data to a holistic "fusion" approach, integrating heterogeneous data sources to create a 360-degree view of a user's mental state.

  • Multimodal Fusion Framework: Integration of Textual (interviews), Acoustic (voice patterns), Visual (facial expressions), and Physiological (EEG/GSR signals) data.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Prioritizing transparency to ensure that AI-generated scores are interpretable by clinical professionals.
  • Domain Adaptation: Utilizing cross-attention mechanisms to understand how demographic and environmental triggers relate to specific disorders.

3. Technical Frameworks (The "How It Works")

  • The Vision Pipeline: Employs CNN-based face detection and expression encoding to capture non-verbal cues of distress.
  • The Acoustic Pipeline: Analyzes prosodic features (pitch, tone, pauses) using audio processing techniques to identify emotional markers.
  • The Physiological Pipeline: Integrates real-time data from Bluetooth-enabled wearables (EEG for brain activity and GSR for skin conductance) to measure physiological stress responses.

4. Deliverables & Progress

Current Milestone: ~40% Completion

  • Accomplished: Dataset curation (publicly available datasets like DAIC-WOZ), baseline model development, and initial fusion logic.
  • In Progress: Real-time validation, web-interface deployment, and mobile app integration for scalable clinical use.
