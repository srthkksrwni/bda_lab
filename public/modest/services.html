MODEST

Multimodal Analysis for Mental Disorder Recognition to Improve Well-being

Our Approach to Healing

The Science of Feeling Seen: Project MODEST is redefining the path to well-being. By integrating Multimodal Analysis for Mental Disorder Recognition, we offer a more comprehensive view of the healing journey. We provide a safe, inclusive sanctuary to unpack complex feelings and restructure your life's narrative.

Whether you are navigating individual growth or relationship dynamics, our clinicians facilitate a "decolonized" approach to therapy—combining cutting-edge recognition technology with ancient strategies for movement and change.

Areas of Focus

  • ADHD
  • Anxiety
  • Bipolar
  • Mood and Personality
  • Poly/Couples Work
  • Depression
  • PTSD
  • Peer Relationships
  • Stress (work, academic, family)

Technical Support Services

For researchers and partner clinicians, we offer technical diagnostic support through our MODEST framework:

  • Digital Biomarker Analysis: Interpretation of EEG and GSR data for clinical research.
  • Speech & Text Screening: Automated sentiment and emotional marker extraction for therapeutic documentation.
  • Early Intervention Alerts: AI-driven scoring for identifying high-risk symptoms in early stages.
"We are building a space where the multimodal (the technical) meets the modest (the humble, human-centric approach), allowing for a radical rediscovery of the self"

Project Identity

Multimodal Analysis for Mental Disorder Recognition to Improve Mental Health and Well-being.An initiative at the BDA Lab, IIIT Allahabad, aimed at integrating AI-driven diagnostics with culturally grounded perspectives to support more accessible, equitable, and context-sensitive mental health solutions
