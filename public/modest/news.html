News & Recent Events

Stay updated with our latest milestones, conferences, and community workshops.

Latest Updates

February 2026

DEXA 2025 Conference Presentation

The MODEST team presented their latest findings on early mental disorder score estimation at the 36th International Conference on Database and Expert Systems Applications (DEXA) in partnership with Springer Nature.

December 2025

IEEE INDICON 2025 Submission

Our research on Explainable AI (XAI) for mental health diagnostics has been accepted for presentation, focusing on building clinician trust through interpretable multimodal fusion.

August 2025

Open Source Mental Health Informatics

We have launched an "Awesome Mental Health Informatics" GitHub repository. This curated collection serves as a premier resource for researchers, featuring state-of-the-art datasets, multimodal fusion algorithms, and pre-trained models for mental health recognition.

Ongoing

Wearable Integration

Integrated Bluetooth-enabled EEG and GSR sensors into the MODEST framework, achieving higher accuracy in real-time stress marker detection.

Upcoming Events

  • MODEST Symposium 2026: A gathering of informatics experts and clinical psychologists to discuss AI in mental health (Dates TBD).
  • Student Counseling Drive: Implementation of the MODEST early-estimation framework in local university clinics for student well-being.
  • Student Workshop:Planning to Host a workshop at IIIT Allahabad for students and faculty to discuss the role of technology in decolonizing mental health and providing inclusive therapeutic support.

Project Identity
